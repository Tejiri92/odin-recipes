World's Best Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is packed with layers of seasoned meat, rich tomato sauce, creamy ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • 3/4 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • Lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. In a large pot, cook sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic until well browned.
  2. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, and water. Simmer for 1.5 hours.
  3. In a separate bowl, combine ricotta cheese with egg and a dash of salt.
  4. Layer the meat sauce, noodles, and cheese mixtures in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 50 minutes. Let stand 15 minutes before serving.
